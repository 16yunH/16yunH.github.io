Current research

World-Action Models for Closed-Loop Robotic Manipulation

Advised by Wenchao Ding

Developing an execution-feedback framework around LingBot-VA on LIBERO-Long to study how task-progress signals and discrepancies between predicted futures and observations can condition the same policy to revise its actions.

Current experiments focus on subtask handoffs: how arm and gripper configurations, reachability, and streaming observation history affect recovery. I use state replay and controlled interventions to distinguish changes in model outputs from task recovery, and investigate how favorable simulated starting states can be reached through safe, executable actions.
